Listening · B1

Announcements and opinions

Understand phone messages, announcements and opinions.

Learn

Listening tips

  • Predict the topic from the question.
  • Listen for reasons: because, so, unfortunately.
  • Check dates and times carefully.

Practice

Listening
Listen, then choose the answer.
Show the audio script

Thanks for calling. Unfortunately, the manager is in a meeting until three. Could you call back later, or I can take a message.

Why can't the caller speak to the manager?

Correct!

The manager is in a meeting until three.

Not quite.

The answer is He is in a meeting. The manager is in a meeting until three.
