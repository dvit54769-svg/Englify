Reading · B1

Phones: benefits and balance

Read a short article and understand main ideas and details.

Learn

Reading tips

  • Find the main idea of each sentence: problems, benefits, advice.
  • Guess new words from the words around them.
  • Words like however and for example show contrast and examples.

Practice

Read the text

Many young people today spend hours on their phones. Some experts say this can cause problems, such as poor sleep and less time with family. However, phones also have benefits: students can learn languages, find information quickly and stay in touch with friends who live far away. The key, experts say, is balance. Setting limits, for example turning off notifications during study time or keeping phones out of the bedroom, can help people enjoy the benefits without the problems.

Reading

What problems can too much phone use cause?

Correct!

Not quite.

The answer is poor sleep and less family time. The text mentions poor sleep and less time with family.
