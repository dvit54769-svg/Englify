Writing · A1

About me and my family

Write short, simple texts about yourself.

Learn

Writing tips

  • Start every sentence with a capital letter.
  • End every sentence with a period.
  • Use simple words: I am, I have, I like.
  • Use and to join two ideas.

Practice

Writing 1

Write at least 15 words. Example: My name is Dara. I am sixteen. I live in Phnom Penh.

Writing 2

Write at least 15 words. Example: I have a mother, a father and one sister.

Writing 3

Write at least 12 words. Example: My favorite food is rice. I eat it every day.
