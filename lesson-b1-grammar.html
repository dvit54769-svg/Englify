Grammar · B1

Perfect tenses, conditionals and passive

Use the present perfect, first conditional, passive voice, modals and relative clauses.

Learn

Present perfect

Use have / has + past participle for experiences and for situations that started in the past and continue now. Use since with a point in time and for with a period.

  • I have lived here since 2020.
  • She has worked here for three years.

First conditional

Use if + present simple, then will + verb, for real possibilities in the future.

  • If it rains, we will stay at home.

Passive voice

Use be + past participle when the action is more important than who did it.

  • The bridge was built in 1995.

Modals and relative clauses

Use must for strong obligation. Use who for people and which for things.

  • You must wear a helmet.
  • The woman who lives next door is a doctor.
