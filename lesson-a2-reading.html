Reading · A2

Dara's job at the café

Read a short story about work and daily life.

Learn

Reading tips

  • Skim the text first to get the main idea.
  • Then scan for the exact detail in the question.
  • Time words (in the morning, last month, next year) tell you when.

Practice

Read the text

Dara works in a small café in Siem Reap. She starts work at seven o'clock in the morning and finishes at three in the afternoon. Last month she learned to make coffee and bake bread. She likes her job because she meets people from many countries. In the evening she studies English for one hour. Next year she wants to travel to Australia.

Reading

Where does Dara work?

Correct!

She works in a small café in Siem Reap.

Not quite.

The answer is in a small café. She works in a small café in Siem Reap.

Reading

What time does she finish work?

Correct!

She finishes at three in the afternoon.

Not quite.

The answer is 3 p.m.. She finishes at three in the afternoon.

Reading

What did she learn last month?

Correct!

Last month she learned to make coffee and bake bread.

Not quite.

The answer is to make coffee and bake bread. Last month she learned to make coffee and bake bread.

Reading

Why does she like her job?

Correct!

She likes it because she meets people from many countries.

Not quite.

The answer is She meets people from many countries. She likes it because she meets people from many countries.

Reading

What does she want to do next year?

Correct!

She wants to travel to Australia next year.

Not quite.

The answer is travel to Australia. She wants to travel to Australia next year.
