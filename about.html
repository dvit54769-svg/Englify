About

About Englify

Englify is a small web-based English learning website with short, structured activities.

About Englify

Students can choose an English level, study short lessons, complete exercises, take quizzes and see their scores. It is inspired by Duolingo, but Englify has its own content, interface and colors.

Purpose

To make English practice more accessible and easier to follow, and to guide learners from one activity to the next without unnecessary complexity.

Learning approach

Learn a little, practice right away, get instant feedback, take a quiz and check your progress. Small steps, big progress.

Features

What you can do on Englify

3 levels

A1, A2 and B1 courses with six lessons each.

Level test

15 questions to find your level.

Listening and speaking

Audio and read-aloud practice.

Saved scores

Your best score for each lesson and quiz.

FAQ

Questions and answers

Is Englify free?

Yes. Englify is a student project. There is no payment or subscription.

Do I need an account?

No. You can learn without an account. Your best scores are saved in this browser.

Where is my progress saved?

In your browser on this device. If you clear your browser data, your progress is removed too.

What do A1, A2 and B1 mean?

They are common levels of English. A1 is beginner, A2 is elementary and B1 is intermediate.

Which browser is best for audio?

Listening audio works in most modern browsers. If you cannot hear it, open "Show the audio script" under the question.
