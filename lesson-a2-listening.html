Listening · A2

Directions, prices and plans

Understand short conversations about everyday situations.

Learn

Listening tips

  • Underline the key word in each question.
  • Numbers and prices can sound similar: 15 and 50.
  • Listen a second time to check your answer.

Which bus goes to the airport?

Correct!
